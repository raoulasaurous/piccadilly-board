    How the sizes relate. EasyFrame's size is the inside of the frame at the back: the backing board and the mount are cut to it. The 6 mm lip covers the outer 6 mm of the mount, so the white you see is the border less 6. On the plug side the border also has to hold the bezel and the plugs: border ≥ bezel − 1 + plug reach + 2 mm clearance (the opening overlaps the bezel by 1 mm). Measured: the moulding, the mount, the monitor's 12 and 7 mm thickness, the 40 mm plug reach. Assumed until you measure: how the black bezel splits round the picture (12 mm each side from the listing's 368 mm width), so measure the plug-side bezel and set it with the slider.
